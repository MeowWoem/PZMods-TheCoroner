[ v1.0.0 - 11.12.2026 ]
Release
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.1 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:	
- Spanish localization
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.2 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- Wounds Overhaul compatibility: when that mod is enabled, its "Practise on the body" option is moved into the Coroner submenu

Fixed:
- Time of death estimate: a very wide range (e.g. "<1h to 2.0 yr") was always counted as correct. The range is now capped (the upper bound can be at most 6x the lower bound). Ranges that are too wide are shown in red as "(too vague)" and can't be submitted, and they don't use up your one attempt on the body.

Changed:
- Time of death XP now scales with the new maximum width: the narrower the correct range, the more XP
- The default range on the timeline is narrower when the window opens
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.3 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- Sandbox options (new "The Coroner" page):
  - Global First Aid XP multiplier
  - Separate XP multipliers for autopsy, time of death and cause of death (combined with the global one)
  - Hand tremor multiplier (0 = perfectly steady hands)
  - Time of death: maximum range width (default 6x)
  - Organs are edible (on by default). When off, organs can't be eaten or added to stews, soups and stir fries, but can still be used for fishing and crafting
  - Infected organs transmit the infection (on by default)
  - Morgue drawers: corpse chance (default 10%) and infected corpse chance (default 50%)

Changed:
- When infected organs are set not to transmit the infection, the external examination no longer warns that the organs are deadly
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.4 - 11.12.2026 ]
Fixed:
- External examination: findings were no longer remembered after closing the window (everything showed "not examined" again). Findings already made are restored.
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.5 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- TooltipLib compatibility: when TooltipLib is enabled, the mod's tooltip lines (organs, chest plate, spent bullets) are registered as a TooltipLib provider instead of hooking the tooltip directly

Fixed:
- Error when hovering the fluid bar of the fluid transfer window (and other tooltips that don't show an inventory item)
- Error spam every tick in TCA_Rot.lua on setups where no other mod defines the "next" function
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.6 - 11.14.2026 ]
Added:
- Morgue drawers can now be picked up, moved and rotated like other furniture (empty both drawers first), or disassembled for scrap
- Morgue drawers can be built from the build menu (Metalworking 5, Electrical 3)
- Beta blockers (vanilla pills) steady your hands during the autopsy minigame. Two new sandbox options: tremor reduction (default 50%) and duration (default 4 in-game hours). Their tooltip shows the effect and the time left
- The thermometer now works on the living: take your own temperature from the inventory, or another player's from the world context menu (they get a confirmation prompt, like the vanilla medical check)
- Digital thermometer: rare loot, can also be crafted (Electrical 2) from a small electronic device, electronics scrap, an electric wire and duct tape or glue. Faster than the mercury thermometer, runs on a regular battery (about 50 readings)
- Placing a corpse on the autopsy table now plays the vanilla "put corpse into container" animation, with the body following the motion. From a corpse on the floor, your character grabs it, walks to the table and places it automatically
- Taking a corpse off the table now plays the vanilla "take corpse out of container" animation, and you keep dragging the body

Changed:
- The autopsy and the external examination can no longer be done in the dark (same rule as reading: a nearby light or a lit flashlight is enough). If the light goes out, the action stops and your progress is saved

Fixed:
- The XP shown after an autopsy step, a time of death or a cause of death estimate now matches the XP actually gained (skill books, profession, traits and the game's XP multiplier were already applied, but not displayed)
