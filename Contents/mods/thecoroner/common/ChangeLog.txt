Release

[ v1.0.1 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:	
- Spanish localization

[ v1.0.2 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- Wounds Overhaul compatibility: when that mod is enabled, its "Practise on the body" option is moved into the Coroner submenu

Fixed:
- Time of death estimate: a very wide range (e.g. "<1h to 2.0 yr") was always counted as correct. The range is now capped (the upper bound can be at most 6x the lower bound). Ranges that are too wide are shown in red as "(too vague)" and can't be submitted, and they don't use up your one attempt on the body.

Changed:
- Time of death XP now scales with the new maximum width: the narrower the correct range, the more XP
- The default range on the timeline is narrower when the window opens
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.3 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- Sandbox options (new "The Coroner" page):
  - Global First Aid XP multiplier
  - Separate XP multipliers for autopsy, time of death and cause of death (combined with the global one)
  - Hand tremor multiplier (0 = perfectly steady hands)
  - Time of death: maximum range width (default 6x)
  - Organs are edible (on by default). When off, organs can't be eaten or added to stews, soups and stir fries, but can still be used for fishing and crafting
  - Infected organs transmit the infection (on by default)
  - Morgue drawers: corpse chance (default 10%) and infected corpse chance (default 50%)

Changed:
- When infected organs are set not to transmit the infection, the external examination no longer warns that the organs are deadly
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.4 - 11.12.2026 ]
Fixed:
- External examination: findings were no longer remembered after closing the window (everything showed "not examined" again). Findings already made are restored.
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.5 - 11.12.2026 ]
Added:
- TooltipLib compatibility: when TooltipLib is enabled, the mod's tooltip lines (organs, chest plate, spent bullets) are registered as a TooltipLib provider instead of hooking the tooltip directly

Fixed:
- Error when hovering the fluid bar of the fluid transfer window (and other tooltips that don't show an inventory item)
- Error spam every tick in TCA_Rot.lua on setups where no other mod defines the "next" function

[ v1.0.6 - 11.14.2026 ]
Added:
- Morgue drawers can now be picked up, moved and rotated like other furniture (empty both drawers first), or disassembled for scrap
- Morgue drawers can be built from the build menu (Metalworking 5, Electrical 3)
- Beta blockers (vanilla pills) steady your hands during the autopsy minigame. Two new sandbox options: tremor reduction (default 50%) and duration (default 4 in-game hours). Their tooltip shows the effect and the time left
- The thermometer now works on the living: take your own temperature from the inventory, or another player's from the world context menu (they get a confirmation prompt, like the vanilla medical check)
- Digital thermometer: rare loot, can also be crafted (Electrical 2) from a small electronic device, electronics scrap, an electric wire and duct tape or glue. Faster than the mercury thermometer, runs on a regular battery (about 50 readings)
- Placing a corpse on the autopsy table now plays the vanilla "put corpse into container" animation, with the body following the motion. From a corpse on the floor, your character grabs it, walks to the table and places it automatically
- Taking a corpse off the table now plays the vanilla "take corpse out of container" animation, and you keep dragging the body

Changed:
- The autopsy and the external examination can no longer be done in the dark (same rule as reading: a nearby light or a lit flashlight is enough). If the light goes out, the action stops and your progress is saved

Fixed:
- The XP shown after an autopsy step, a time of death or a cause of death estimate now matches the XP actually gained (skill books, profession, traits and the game's XP multiplier were already applied, but not displayed)
[ ------ ]

[ v1.0.7 - 11.14.2026 ]
Added:
- Wound Expansion compatibility: its wounds, conditions and scars (abrasions, punctures, frostbite, sprains, dislocations, concussions, internal bleeding, sepsis...) now show up in the external examination of a dead player. The less obvious ones need a higher First Aid level to be found
- Casualties Undead compatibility: amputations, prostheses, missing eyes, broken ribs, infected wounds, internal conditions and overdoses are recorded on a dead player's body and can be found during the external examination. The Casualties Undead dissection option is hidden, the autopsy replaces it
- The Only Cure compatibility: amputated arms, the state of the stump (open, cauterized or healed), prostheses and tourniquets show up in the external examination
- New causes of death when one of these mods is enabled: blood loss, sepsis, exposure (cold or heat), overdose and organ failure. Zombies and morgue corpses can also show injuries and causes of death from these mods
- Diseased organs (Casualties Undead): organs damaged before death keep their lesions when harvested. The tooltip shows them from First Aid 3, and diseased organs can't be used in recipes that need a healthy organ

Changed:
- Amputated limbs are now hidden on the 3D body of the examination and autopsy windows, and Wound Expansion skin marks are shown
- The examination markers adapt to missing body parts: rigor is tested on the other arm or on a leg when an arm is missing (impossible if no limb is left), the eye test moves to the remaining eye, and on an amputated limb only the highest missing part keeps its wound marker

[ v1.0.8 - 11.16.2026 ]
Added:
- The Y incision accepts more tools: a straight razor or a small knife, any sharp knife (including stone knives and shivs) or a machete can be used instead. The cruder the blade, the more your hand shakes and the narrower the path: the skin edges are ragged and tear more easily, a slip on the abdomen can nick the stomach, intestines or bladder, a slip can also cut your hand deeply, the incision gives less XP and the blade wears out. A dull blade makes all of this worse (the scalpel too), and the Butchering skill reduces the penalties. Swords, scissors and disposable razors can't be used
- Mod Options (new "The Coroner" page): size of the autopsy and examination windows. Auto adapts them to your screen (about 135% in 1440p, 200% in 4K), or pick from 100% to 250%. The text gets larger with the windows (can be turned off). The minigame plays exactly the same at every size
- Wounds Overhaul compatibility: open fractures, third-degree burns, skin grafts, sutured wounds (neat or poorly stitched), tourniquets, sepsis, blood loss and overdoses are recorded on a dead player's body and can be found during the external examination. Sepsis, blood loss and overdose can now be concluded as causes of death
- Wounds Overhaul: liver and kidneys damaged by drugs or alcohol keep their lesions when harvested during the autopsy
- Wounds Overhaul: bullets taken out with its "Take out a bullet" practice are no longer found again during the autopsy, and bullets recovered during the autopsy can no longer be taken out with its practice
- Blood typing: determine a corpse's blood group from the Coroner submenu with the Wounds Overhaul blood typing kit (one test per use). The reading can be wrong at low First Aid levels, so type it again to be sure. A dead player keeps the blood group they had while alive
- Portuguese, Korean and Russian localizations
- For modders: TCA.Autopsy.registerIncisionBlade(itemType, group) lets other mods declare their own incision blades

Changed:
- Names now match the base game in English, French and Spanish: specimen jars ("Specimen Jar - Heart"), body parts in the Wounds tab, skill and item names in tooltips and sandbox options

[ v1.0.9 - 11.17.2026 ]
Added:
- Recipe to turn electric sheep shears into an oscillating saw (Electrical 5): screwdriver and file (kept), a small saw blade or a hacksaw blade, electronics scrap, an electric wire, screws and duct tape or glue. The shears' battery charge carries over to the saw
- Psychological consequences of eating human organs (sandbox options, on by default): stress and unhappiness that linger for a few hours, the first time is the worst and can turn your stomach, and it wears off as you get used to it. Fear of Blood, Cowardly and Weak Stomach make it worse; Brave, Iron Gut, Desensitized, doctors and nurses take it better
- New Cannibal trait, gained after a number of human meals (default 10): eating human flesh now comforts you, but you get cravings after a day without it
- Supported cannibalism mods take over: with HarvestZ's Cannibalism, the organs count as human meat for its insanity; with Cannibalism in Zombie Apocalypse, cooked organs ease its cravings. The Coroner's own effects are then turned off
- Organs hidden in dishes (sandbox option, on by default): a human organ added to a stew, soup or stir fry passes for ground beef in the dish name and its ingredients. Only the cook knows (tooltip line), and experienced characters can grow suspicious (First Aid 7 or Cooking 8 by default, adjustable). Someone who eats it without knowing suffers no psychological effects
- An infected organ in a dish infects whoever eats it, even when the dish is shared out into bowls, and without showing a poison icon
- Zombie Virus Vaccine compatibility, first patch (sandbox option, on by default): its autopsy and its morgue table placement are replaced by The Coroner menu > Zombie Virus Vaccine
  - Collect Blood Samples: same tools, chances, experience and options as Zombie Virus Vaccine, on the ground or on a table
  - Collect a Body Part: brain, skull, teeth and bones, each once per body, and the body stays. No more choosing between the brain and the skull. Requires a scalpel and any saw, including the bone saw and the oscillating saw
  - Works on any table, including the ones already on the map
  - When the option is off, Zombie Virus Vaccine works exactly as usual
  - A message on the main menu lists the changes the first time both mods are enabled
- Debug mode: hidden stats window (cannibalism, infection, other mods) and an option to give organs in any state, healthy or infected

Changed:
- Diminishing First Aid XP from the mod as your level rises (sandbox options): 100% up to level 3, then 10% less per level, never below 20%. Set to 0 to turn it off
- All debug options are grouped in a [DEBUG] submenu of the Coroner menu
- Corpses on the floor are now found around the mouse cursor: furniture in front of a body no longer hides the Coroner options
