description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Because hacking away at your own arm with a hunting knife in the bathroom just isn't a sustainable long-term workout routine for grinding First Aid, it's time to let someone else do the bleeding.
description=
description=The Coroner: Autopsy Mod brings a complete forensic pathology system to Project Zomboid, turning corpse management into the ultimate, hands-on training ground for your medical skills. Examine body temperatures, track exact wound histories, perform precision surgical mini-games, and harvest organs... which are edible, though eating a zed's liver is strongly discouraged... unless you're looking to poison your cheating husband. Just remember to pair a nice liver with some fava beans and a bottle of Chianti.
description=
description=[b]Getting started:[/b] right-click a body or a table and open the [b]Coroner[/b] submenu. The video above walks through everything.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Autopsy on any table:[/b] lay a body on any table at least 2 tiles long.
description=[*] [b]Morgue drawers:[/b] a power switch, bodies kept cold when a generator runs them. Pick them up and move them like any furniture, or build your own (Metalworking 5, Electrical 3).
description=[*] [b]Surgical mini-game:[/b] Y incision with a Scalpel (or, if you must, a Straight Razor, a Knife or a Machete: cruder blades mean shakier hands, ragged skin, nicked organs and deep cuts), skin pulled back with Forceps, 12 ribs cut with Bolt Cutters or a Saw, Chest Plate lifted, then 11 organs freed and pulled out in order. Your hands shake depending on your First Aid level and your moodles: slip, and you cut yourself. Beta Blockers (vanilla pills) steady your hands for a few hours.
description=[*] [b]You need light:[/b] no autopsy or examination in the dark. A nearby lamp or a lit flashlight is enough.
description=[*] [b]External examination:[/b] read the clues on a 3D model of the body: temperature, eyes, rigor mortis, livor, decomposition and insect activity (eggs, maggots, pupae). Then estimate the time of death on a timeline: the narrower your correct range, the more XP.
description=[*] [b]Wounds & cause of death:[/b] every hit is recorded (blunt, blade, axe, spear, gunshot with caliber, falls, fire, bites...). Examine 17 body parts, tell pre-mortem from post-mortem injuries, and conclude on the cause of death. Missing limbs and eyes are taken into account.
description=[*] [b]Thermometers for the living:[/b] take your own temperature, or another player's (they get a confirmation prompt, like the vanilla medical check) to spot hypothermia or a fever.
description=[*] [b]Organs & crafting:[/b] organs are food that rots, can be cooked and can be infected. Turn them into Specimen Jars, Catgut, a Bladder Canteen or Liver Tan, and saw the Chest Plate into Human Bones for vanilla bone recipes.
description=[*] [b]Cannibalism:[/b] eating human organs brings stress and guilt, the first time is the worst, and you get used to it. Eat enough and you gain the Cannibal trait: comfort when you eat, cravings when you don't. Organs added to a stew pass for ground beef: only the cook knows, and an infected organ infects the whole table.
description=[*] [b]Blood typing:[/b] determine a corpse's blood group with the Wounds Overhaul Blood Typing Kit (requires Wounds Overhaul). Low First Aid can misread it, so test twice.
description=[*] [b]Knowledge progression:[/b] three Forensic Medicine books (or a high First Aid level) unlock a field guide inside the examination window.
description=[*] [b]First Aid training:[/b] XP for every autopsy step, every correct time of death estimate and every correct cause of death, instead of self-harming, with diminishing returns as you level up. Skill books, professions and traits apply as usual.
description=[/list]
