description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Because hacking away at your own arm with a hunting knife in the bathroom just isn't a sustainable long-term workout routine for grinding First Aid, it's time to let someone else do the bleeding.
description=
description=The Coroner: Autopsy Mod brings a complete forensic pathology system to Project Zomboid Build 42, turning corpse management into the ultimate, hands-on training ground for your medical skills. Examine body temperatures, track exact wound histories, perform precision surgical mini-games, and harvest organs... which are fully edible, though eating a zed's liver is strongly discouraged... unless you're looking to poison your cheating husband. Just remember to pair a nice liver with some fava beans and a bottle of Chianti.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Autopsy Table & Morgue Drawers:[/b] Place corpses onto custom autopsy tables or utilize functional morgue drawers featuring refrigerated preservation mechanics.
description=[*] [b]First Aid Training & Forensics:[/b] Level up your medical expertise through hands-on autopsies, external examinations, rigor mortis tracking, and insect activity analysis instead of self-harming.
description=[*] [b]Surgical Mini-Game:[/b] Perform step-by-step surgical procedures using scalpels, forceps, bone saws, and bolt cutters.
description=[*] [b]Organ Harvesting & Crafting:[/b] Extract organs that function as food items, preserve biological specimens in jars, and process human bones for specialized crafting recipes.
description=[*] [b]Wound & Cause of Death Tracking:[/b] Log pre and post-mortem trauma (gunshots, lacerations, bites) to scientifically determine how the deceased met their end.
description=[*] [b]Knowledge Progression:[/b] Unlock advanced forensic tiers dynamically through dedicated medical books or by raising your First Aid skill.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Added Items[/h2]
description=[b]Tools[/b][list]
description=[*] [b]Thermometer:[/b] mercury thermometer to take a body's temperature during the external examination.
description=[*] [b]Bone saw:[/b] manual saw made for cutting ribs. Also counts as a small saw in crafting recipes.
description=[*] [b]Oscillating bone saw:[/b] the best tool for the ribs. Runs on a battery that you can insert or remove.
description=[*] [b]Formalin:[/b] bottle of preserving fluid used to make specimen jars. Deadly if you drink it.
description=[/list]
