description=[h2]Description[/h2]
description=Because hacking away at your own arm with a hunting knife in the bathroom just isn't a sustainable long-term workout routine for grinding First Aid, it's time to let someone else do the bleeding.
description=
description=The Coroner: Autopsy Mod brings a complete forensic pathology system to Project Zomboid Build 42, turning corpse management into the ultimate, hands-on training ground for your medical skills. Examine body temperatures, track exact wound histories, perform precision surgical mini-games, and harvest organs... which are edible, though eating a zed's liver is strongly discouraged... unless you're looking to poison your cheating husband. Just remember to pair a nice liver with some fava beans and a bottle of Chianti.
description=
description=[b]Getting started:[/b] right-click a body or a table and open the [b]Coroner[/b] submenu. The video above walks through everything.
description=
description=[h2]Features[/h2][list]
description=[*] [b]Autopsy on any table:[/b] lay a body on any table at least 2 tiles long, straight from the floor or carried. Morgue drawers get a power switch and keep bodies cold when a generator runs them.
description=[*] [b]Surgical mini-game:[/b] Y incision with a scalpel, skin pulled back with forceps, 12 ribs cut with bolt cutters or a saw, chest plate lifted, then 11 organs freed and pulled out in order. Your hands shake depending on your First Aid level and your moodles: slip, and you cut yourself.
description=[*] [b]External examination:[/b] read the clues on a 3D model of the body: temperature, eyes, rigor mortis, livor, decomposition and insect activity (eggs, maggots, pupae). Then estimate the time of death on a timeline: the narrower your correct range, the more XP.
description=[*] [b]Wounds & cause of death:[/b] every hit is recorded (blunt, blade, axe, spear, gunshot with caliber, falls, fire, bites...). Examine 17 body parts, tell pre-mortem from post-mortem injuries, and conclude on the cause of death.
description=[*] [b]Organs & crafting:[/b] organs are food that rots, can be cooked and can be infected. Turn them into specimen jars, catgut, a bladder canteen or tanning paste, recover bullets from them, and saw the chest plate into human bones for vanilla bone recipes.
description=[*] [b]Knowledge progression:[/b] three forensic medicine books (or a high First Aid level) unlock a field guide inside the examination window.
description=[*] [b]First Aid training:[/b] XP for every autopsy step, every correct time of death estimate and every correct cause of death, instead of self-harming.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox Options[/h2]
description=A dedicated "The Coroner" page lets you tune the mod: global XP multiplier plus separate multipliers for autopsy, time of death and cause of death, hand tremor strength, maximum width of the time of death estimate, edible organs on/off, infectious organs on/off, and the chance of finding bodies (and infected ones) in morgue drawers.
description=
